# Implementation plan: Galecrest Isle

## Source-of-truth decisions

The implementation follows `docs/spec/build-a-copyright-safe-browser-3d-svr3tgr.md`. It is one original, continuous 10–15 minute floating-island level called **Galecrest Isle**, delivered entirely from `mario64/` as a static TypeScript/Vite/Three.js application. Its high-fidelity target is expressive, predictable land movement—not copied physics constants, geometry, presentation, or audiovisual material. The player is an original faceted non-human wind-runner in a new pale-rock, amber-grass, timber, wind-ribbon, and crystalline-machine setting.

The fixed progression is: activate three foothill beacons in any order; collect five ascent motes after the first gate opens; then bait an original crystal-and-wind guardian into arena conductors and attack its exposed core three times before claiming a crest. Checkpoints follow the beacon and mote objectives. Completed objective state and collected items survive defeat, while the current encounter resets. The world has an original landing shelf, three branching foothill routes, a gated switchback ascent, and a summit arena; it must be designed from scratch rather than traced from the cited inspiration.

The required land moves are walk/run, single/double/timed triple jump, side flip, crouch backflip, crouch-running long jump, wall kick, dive-to-slide, aerial ground pound, ledge grab/hang/climb, slope traversal/sliding, moving-platform carry, fall damage, knockback, and recovery. Mandatory routes do not demand advanced moves. The camera is a smoothed third-person orbit with obstruction handling, zoom, recenter, pitch clamp, inversion, sensitivity, and a materially calmer reduced-motion mode.

Keyboard/mouse, standard gamepad, and landscape multitouch must each be independently sufficient and remain active simultaneously. Defaults and combination semantics are those in spec section 7: strongest-source input, clamped movement, correct multi-source press/release behavior, most-recent camera source, remappable persistent keyboard/gamepad bindings, and clearing on pause, blur, disconnect, or cancellation. Touch includes safe areas, scaling, multitouch, rotation handling, pinch zoom, and double-tap recenter. Pointer lock is optional.

Combat includes the charger, hopping spitter, rooted crystal bloom, and three-hit summit guardian described by the spec. The game has health, readable telegraphs, knockback/invulnerability, optional shards/health drops, no lives, and checkpoint recovery. Pause and persistent settings cover controls, master/music/effects volume, sensitivities, inverted Y, touch scale, assist mode, and reduced motion. Assist reduces damage and expands timing/recovery generosity without removing objectives.

All shipped content must be original to the repository or redistribution/modification-compatible and recorded in `mario64/ATTRIBUTION.md`; production fetches no remote gameplay assets. The target matrix is current Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge desktop plus iOS Safari and Android Chrome. Adaptive quality targets roughly 60 fps on a representative 2022 laptop and 30 fps on a representative 2022 phone, and compressed initial transfer must remain below 15 MB. Browser/device/performance results are evidence to record, not universal guarantees.

## Delivery slices

### t1 — Playable cross-input movement playground

Create the complete `mario64/` Vite/TypeScript/Three.js project and a small original landing-shelf playground that builds and runs over static HTTP. Implement the fixed-step simulation seam, basic collision, camera-relative walk/run and single jump, a faceted placeholder-quality-but-original player presentation, orbit/zoom/recenter camera with obstruction handling, start/controls/HUD/pause surfaces, WebGL failure handling, and the normalized input architecture. Keyboard/mouse, standard gamepad, landscape multitouch, and mixed sources must all move, orbit, jump, and pause in this increment; pointer lock must remain optional. Add the settings storage seam and working sensitivity, Y inversion, touch sensitivity/scale, reduced-motion, and assist settings, including remapping scaffolding with conflict-safe required bindings. Handle blur, visibility, disconnect, touch cancellation, resize, and orientation without stuck input. Add unit/integration tests for normalization, lifecycle clearing, settings persistence/fallback, and deterministic basic movement, plus README commands. This slice ends as a genuinely playable movement playground, not merely framework code.

### t2 — Original traversal island and complete move set

Replace/extend the playground into the complete original Galecrest Isle traversal space: safe shelf, three distinguishable branching foothill loops, visibly gated switchback ascent, moving platforms and wall-kick/ledge challenges, safe recovery areas, and summit arena. Do not trace reference geometry, silhouette, landmarks, or compositions. Implement every remaining movement, collision, and recovery behavior from spec section 4: acceleration/turning/skid, analog speed, air control, slopes/sliding, jump chain, side flip, backflip, long jump, wall kick, dive-to-slide, ground pound, ledge hang/climb, moving-platform carry, landing/fall states, buffered/coyote jumps, and predictable void recovery. Tune a standard route so every future required location is reachable without advanced techniques while adding optional skill shortcuts. Extend deterministic player-simulation tests across each move and collision fixture. The result is an explorable, coherent single-level traversal sandbox even before objectives are installed.

### t3 — Beacon and ascent objective run

Add the pure run-state reducer and integrate the first two complete objectives. Place three uniquely identified beacons on distinct foothill branches, activate each idempotently with shape-plus-color world/HUD feedback, keep the ascent gate closed until all three activate, then transition without loading and establish checkpoint one. Place five one-shot motes across ordinary ascent, moving-platform, switchback, and wall-kick routes; keep the summit gate closed until all five are collected, then transition and establish checkpoint two. Add optional shards, objective guidance, timer behavior, duplicate-event protection, durable progress across damage/defeat/void falls, and correct transient reset/respawn behavior. Provide stable accessible UI selectors and test-only landmark identifiers that are inert in production. Unit-test reducer/checkpoint semantics and add browser integration coverage for out-of-order beacons, both gates, five motes, and recovery during objectives one and two. This slice is a playable collection-and-ascent run ending at the unlocked summit.

### t4 — Enemies, guardian, and start-to-finish completion

Implement deterministic encounter seams and the three original small enemies: telegraphing ground charger, hopping slow-projectile spitter, and rooted short-range hazard bloom. Add readable damage, knockback, post-hit invulnerability, health pickups/shard drops, valid stomp/dive/ground-pound defeats, fall damage, zero-health recovery, and phase-appropriate enemy reset without making combat a progression grind. Build the summit encounter around enabled conductors: telegraph an attack, require it to hit a conductor, expose the core for a finite window, accept at most one valid movement-attack hit per exposure, progress through three readable phases, and fully reset guardian hits after objective-three defeat. After hit three, stop danger, release an original crest, and on claim freeze play/timer and show accurate time and shard count. Add deterministic encounter tests and a real-gate browser smoke test from start through all objectives and completion. The result is the first complete game loop.

### t5 — Production polish, accessibility, provenance, and validation

Bring the complete loop to release acceptance without changing its source-of-truth design. Finish coherent procedural/original low-poly art, shape-and-color cues, responsive HUD/menus, synthesized original audio/music with immediate master/music/effects controls, full keyboard and gamepad remapping UI, touch safe-area/multitouch polish, controls overlay, restart confirmation, storage/audio/pointer-lock fallbacks, reduced-motion behavior, and assist effects. Implement adaptive capped-pixel-ratio/shadow/effect/detail tiers without removing gameplay cues or colliders. Create a complete `ATTRIBUTION.md` covering internal provenance, dependencies, and any permitted external content; add emitted-asset inventory, remote-gameplay-request check, and compressed-transfer budget check. Run and record the specified desktop/mobile browser, independent-input and mixed-input, lifecycle, settings, complete-run console, performance, resolution/quality-tier, and static-production validation in repository documentation. Fix issues found, ensure all automated tests/build/preview commands pass, and audit player-facing names, visuals, geometry, text, and audio for copyright safety. No placeholders, debug dependency, or undocumented acceptance gap may remain.

## Dependency order

`t1` establishes the runtime and cross-input simulation. `t2` depends on it for the complete movement/world substrate. `t3` depends on the finished traversable world for meaningful objective placement and checkpoints. `t4` depends on the objective chain and summit access to deliver completion. `t5` validates and polishes the complete game. These dependencies are deliberately linear because each later slice integrates with and tests the behavior delivered immediately before it; each slice nevertheless ends in a runnable experience.
