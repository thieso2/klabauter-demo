# Attribution and provenance

## Repository-created content

All player-facing names, text, track layouts, kart/pilot designs, item designs, UI styling, and interaction design are authored for the Turbo Loop Cup in this repository. Visuals are drawn procedurally with Canvas 2D; there are no imported models, textures, images, fonts, or audio samples.

No generative service or third-party asset library was used. Production loads no remote gameplay content.
